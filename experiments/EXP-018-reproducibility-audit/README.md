> **Title scope (2026-10-11 audit).** The title preserves the historical terminology. The audit distinguishes within-invocation repetition variability, nominal recorded-configuration cross-run spread, and true fully controlled same-configuration variability. The latter was not isolated by the legacy data. "Session-to-session" is kept as historical shorthand: the archive carries no explicit session IDs.

## Research question

How variable were Argine v1 generation-speed measurements within one invocation and across records that matched the configuration fields preserved by the historical logs?

*(Original wording, kept as history: "How reproducible are Argine's speed measurements, within and across runs of the same configuration?")*

## Hypothesis

[HYPOTHESIS] (Argine v1) Repetitions within a run differ by 1-4% and runs of the same curve hours apart by up to 5%.

*Audit reading (2026-10-11):*
- The low-variance assumption is falsified.
- Within-run CV reaches ≈6.43% (0.06433166).
- Records matching the historical grouping key show ≈15-16% min-to-max range spreads (30B: ≈15.36%, ≈16.04%).
- A supplementary 80B raw comparison differs by ≈19% (7.10 → 5.74).
- This does not establish pure stochastic variance under fully controlled identical configurations.

## Setup

All benchmark / sweep records grouped by identical plan (model, quantisation, binary fingerprint, threads, store, expert budget, context, prompt). Within-run CV of the repetitions and between-run spread of the medians.

*[Historical wording, narrowed by the 2026-10-11 audit: "grouped by identical plan" overstates what the grouping key encodes.]*

*Audited setup (2026-10-11):*
- The builder (`tools/build_historical_data.py`, read only, not executed) selects `benchmark` and `benchmark_sweep_budget` records that have generation repetitions and groups them by the **builder nominal grouping key**:
  `(model.name, model.quantization, runtime.build, plan.threads, bool(plan.expert_store), expert budget = expert_budget or plan.expert_cache_budget, plan.ctx, prompt_chars)`.
  Keys with fewer than two records are dropped.
- Fields visibly present in the archived records but **omitted from the key**:
  - read mode (`modo_lettura`), reported afterwards only in the `read_modes` column, so synchronous, block and asynchronous runs can share one nominal group;
  - backend (`plan.backend` / `runtime.backend`), which is not encoded by the build label (`b11214` covers both vulkan and cpu records);
  - `plan.gpu_layers`, `plan.repack`, `plan.parallel`, `plan.llama_verbose`;
  - record type (`event`: sweep vs benchmark) and `n_predict`.
- PRETOUCH state is absent from these historical logs, so it cannot be part of any key.
- Per group the builder writes:
  - `between_run_spread = (max(medians) - min(medians)) / min(medians)`, a descriptive min-to-max relative range. It is not a standard deviation, a cross-session CV, a confidence interval or a formal estimate of a noise distribution;
  - `within_run_cv_max`, the largest per-invocation CV (sample SD / mean of the repetitions inside one invocation);
  - `sessions = len(rr)`, the grouped-record count.
- `results.csv` is therefore a historical nominal-key aggregation, not a fully fingerprinted reproducibility table. Rows are "records matching the historical grouping key", not "identical configurations".

## Result

[MEASURED] Same binary (3cac5531), same model and budget (80B, 4 GiB), eight minutes apart: median 7.10 tok/s (sweep) vs 5.74 (benchmark): -19%. Same binary 78ee4096, 30B, 4 GiB, five runs over ten hours: 5.34 to 6.16 (15%). Same binary 757a17a4, 30B store 6 GiB, three minutes apart: 9.62 vs 8.29 (16%). Within-run CV up to 5.8%.

*[Historical result text, kept as recorded and narrowed below by the 2026-10-11 audit. The 80B pair is not a results.csv row, the 30B groups are nominal recorded-configuration groups, and "within-run CV up to 5.8%" is not reproduced by results.csv (maximum 0.06433166).]*

*Audited results (2026-10-11), by evidence class:*

**A. Within-invocation repetition variability.** [MEASURED]
- The maximum builder-recorded `within_run_cv_max` is 0.06433166 ≈ 6.43%: the 8B invocation of 2026-09-27T16:16:20 (cpu backend; repetitions 7.48 / 7.21 / 6.59 tok/s).
- This CV is computed from repetitions inside one benchmark invocation, so it stays valid even though the 8B between-record grouping is confounded (class D).
- The strongest provenance-clean evidence against the historical low-variance assumption is that at least one archived benchmark invocation had a within-run repetition CV of ≈6.43%, above the historical 1-4% expectation.
- Many invocations are much tighter: the 14B group's maximum is 1.09%, and the four `3cac5531` groups' maxima are 1.7-2.2%.

**B. Nominal legacy recorded-configuration groups.** [MEASURED, LEGACY RECORDED-CONFIGURATION]
- 30B, store 6 GiB, `llama-noomp@757a17a4`: 8.29 → 9.62 tok/s, `between_run_spread` 0.16043426 ≈ 16.04%; two records about three minutes apart (20:29:08, 20:32:29); `read_modes = unrecorded`.
- 30B, store 4 GiB, `llama-noomp@78ee4096`: 5.34 → 6.16 tok/s, 0.15355805 ≈ 15.36%; five records spanning about ten hours (2026-10-02T23:21:45 → 2026-10-03T09:20:42); `read_modes = unrecorded`.
- Under the configuration fields preserved by Argine v1, nominally matched records differed by ≈16.0% and ≈15.4%. Missing historical runtime flags (read mode, PRETOUCH) prevent determining how much of this spread is true run-to-run variability versus hidden configuration difference. These groups are not proven fully identical runtime configurations.
- The other unrecorded-read-mode groups (30B `78ee4096` at 1.5 and 3 GiB: ≈8.7% and ≈7.9%; 30B `ecd06508` at 6 GiB: ≈6.3%) have the same recorded-configuration scope.

**C. Supplementary 80B raw comparison (not a results.csv row).** [MEASURED, SUPPLEMENTARY RAW COMPARISON]
- 2026-10-03T13:42:06, `benchmark_sweep_budget`, Qwen3-Next-80B Q3_K, `llama-noomp@3cac5531`, 4 GiB expert budget: median 7.10 tok/s; `modo_lettura = asincrono` recorded.
- 2026-10-03T13:50:24, `benchmark`, same model, binary and 4 GiB budget: median 5.74 tok/s; the record has no read-mode field.
- Shared: model, binary, expert budget, threads 4, context 4096, 77 prompt tokens and 128 generated tokens. Different: event type, read-mode provenance, `prompt_chars` (null vs 304) and the planner's total `budget` field.
- The pair is absent from results.csv: the builder key includes `prompt_chars`, so the two records fall under different keys, each with a single record, and are dropped.
- Denominator conventions:
  - 5.74 / 7.10 − 1 = −19.15% ≈ −19.2%, the change relative to the earlier 7.10 (the historical "−19%");
  - (7.10 − 5.74) / 5.74 = 23.69% ≈ 23.7% under the builder's `(max − min) / min` convention.
  The two are not mixed.
- Each invocation is internally tight (within-run CV ≈1.4% and ≈3.6%).
- The ≈19% contrast motivated the reproducibility concern, but it cannot be decomposed into cross-time variation versus unrecorded runtime configuration. It is not a controlled same-configuration replication.

**D. Excluded / confounded builder groups.**
- 8B `b11214`, 6.89 → 8.34, 0.21044993 ≈ 21.0%: confounded builder grouping; excluded from reproducibility inference. The four records mix a vulkan run (37 GPU layers) with cpu runs, and repack unset / true / false. Its individual within-run CVs remain valid (class A).
- 30B `llama-noomp@3cac5531`, `read_modes = asincrono blocco sincrono`: 1.5 GiB ≈10.8%, 3 GiB ≈6.1%, 2 GiB ≈5.1%, 4 GiB ≈2.2%. Each row mixes deliberately different read-mode treatments (EXP-004), so these are not reproducibility spreads. They are excluded from reproducibility inference and show only that the historical grouping logic was too coarse.

**E. Tight control example.**
- 14B `llama-noomp@ecd06508`: 3.81 → 3.83, 0.0052493438 ≈ 0.5%. Used only to show heterogeneity: the archive does not support a universal statement that cross-run differences are always 15-19%.

## Interpretation

[FALSIFIED, HISTORICAL / RECORDED-CONFIGURATION-SCOPED]
- The historical ≤5%-style reproducibility assumption cannot be supported by the archive and is operationally falsified.
- Within-invocation CV can exceed the expected 1-4% range (maximum ≈6.43%).
- Records matching the historical grouping key can differ by ≈15-16% (30B: ≈15.36% and ≈16.04%).
- A supplementary 80B comparison differed by ≈19% (relative to 7.10).
- Incomplete runtime provenance prevents interpreting these values as a universal stochastic session noise distribution. Missing runtime flags are an observed provenance limitation.
- Possible uncontrolled contributors include thermal state, background load and cache/system state; EXP-018 does not decompose them.
- The main methodological lesson is stronger provenance, interleaving/rotation and replication, not a universal 15% threshold. For performance comparisons where small effects matter, Argine2's protocol interleaves/rotates configurations within a measurement session to reduce cross-time confounding (METHODOLOGY section 7) and records runtime flags in every manifest (section 8). EXP-018 does not prove interleaving to be the only valid design.
- The historical ~15% rule is retained only as a conservative guardrail for interpreting un-interleaved legacy Argine v1 comparisons with incomplete provenance. It is not a universal significance threshold. Modern Argine2 comparisons rely on interleaving/rotation, complete fingerprints and within-cell replication/uncertainty rather than this cutoff.
- EXP-018 does not establish an intrinsic 15-19% noise floor, controlled same-configuration variance of that size, that cross-time differences below 15% are false, or that all configurations vary by 15-19%.

[STATUS, HISTORICAL] **Audit closure (2026-10-11).** EXP-018 remains FALSIFIED with recorded-configuration / legacy-provenance scope. No rerun is required for historical closure.

*[Historical interpretation, kept as recorded and narrowed above by the 2026-10-11 audit: "[FALSIFIED] Between-run dispersion reaches 15-19%, well above 5%. Part of it is unrecorded configuration (PRETOUCH and the read mode were not logged before 2026-10-03, and the 80B benchmark record lacks the read mode); part is unexplained (thermal state, background load, page cache). Consequences: (1) every comparison must be interleaved inside one session (section 27); (2) every manifest must record runtime flags (section 28); (3) differences below ~15% between sessions are not established."]*

## Limitations

[LIMITATION] Groups are small (2-5 runs); background load and temperature were never recorded.

1. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Historical observational re-analysis of archived logs, not a preregistered reproducibility experiment.
2. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Groups are small (2-5 records).
3. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] No explicit session IDs exist in the archived records or in the builder's calculation.
4. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The CSV column `sessions` is `len(rr)` (grouped-record count), not a count of verified sessions.
5. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The builder grouping key is incomplete: it does not encode the full runtime configuration.
6. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Read mode (`modo_lettura`) is omitted from the grouping key and only reported afterwards in `read_modes`.
7. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Therefore mixed sync/block/async rows exist (the four `llama-noomp@3cac5531` rows); they are treatment mixtures, not replications.
8. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Before 2026-10-03 the read mode was entirely unrecorded (`read_modes = unrecorded`), including both highlighted 30B groups.
9. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] PRETOUCH state is not preserved in any of the 33 grouped records.
10. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Backend (vulkan vs cpu, 37 vs 0 GPU layers) and repack (unset/true/false) differences contaminate the 8B group.
11. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The 80B 7.10 / 5.74 pair is not a results.csv matched group (its `prompt_chars` values, null vs 304, put the two records under different builder keys).
12. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The 80B read-mode provenance is asymmetric (`asincrono` recorded for the sweep record; field absent from the benchmark record); the two records are also of different event types.
13. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Temperature was not recorded.
14. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Background load was not controlled or recorded.
15. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Page-cache and wider system state were not controlled or recorded.
16. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] `(max - min) / min` is a descriptive range, not a statistical variance, cross-run CV or confidence interval; with 2-5 records it is sensitive to a single outlying record.
17. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Only one reference machine.
18. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Time separation varies widely across comparisons (about 3 minutes, about 8 minutes, about 10 hours), so the spreads are not comparable as one cross-time quantity.
19. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The 14B group (≈0.5%) shows that cross-run spread is heterogeneous across models/configurations; no single dispersion value applies to the archive.
20. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The historical binaries and configurations predate Argine2's complete fingerprinting (no binary SHA-256, incomplete runtime flags).
21. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] The historical result text's "within-run CV up to 5.8%" is not reproduced by results.csv, whose maximum `within_run_cv_max` is 0.06433166; the results.csv value is used by this audit.
22. [LIMITATION, historical audit 2026-10-11] Possible uncontrolled contributors (thermal state, background load, cache/system state) were not experimentally isolated; EXP-018 does not decompose them.
