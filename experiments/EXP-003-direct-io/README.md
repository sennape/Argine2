## Research question

Is the storage device or the access path the bottleneck when experts are streamed from NVMe?

## Hypothesis

[HYPOTHESIS] (original, later falsified) Disk bandwidth is the bottleneck. [HYPOTHESIS] (replacement) The page-fault path, not the device, limits mmap streaming; explicit aligned reads into memory we commit ourselves are both faster and make the RAM budget enforceable.

## Setup

`argine diskbench` on the 30B GGUF (cold cache): sequential, mmap page faults and direct (FILE_FLAG_NO_BUFFERING) reads of 896 KiB slices at 1-32 threads; `argine store bench` on four read paths.

## Commands

```bat
argine diskbench C:\modelli\Qwen_Qwen3-30B-A3B-Q4_K_M.gguf
argine store bench MODEL
```

## Raw data

`raw/records.jsonl` — the exact records of Argine's `runs.jsonl` used here (sanitised: host and user name only). Full log: `data/raw/argine-v1/runs.jsonl`. `results.csv` is regenerated by `python tools/build_historical_data.py`.

## Result

[MEASURED] One thread, 896 KiB slices, cold: mmap page faults 309.8 MB/s (2.96 ms latency) vs direct reads 1254-1324 MB/s (0.69-0.73 ms): 4.0-4.3x. Sequential 8 MiB reads: 3305-3678 MB/s. Direct reads scale with readers (2 threads 2899 MB/s, 3 threads 3711 MB/s on 2026-10-03).

[MEASURED, added by the 2026-10-11 audit; already in `results.csv`] The 2026-10-01 diskbench session (one reader, 896 KiB, cold) gave mmap 524.5 MB/s vs direct 1665.8 MB/s (3.2×) and sequential 3677.7 MB/s. The single-reader comparison was therefore measured in three sessions (direct 1254–1666 MB/s), with mmap in two of them (310–525 MB/s). The direction replicates; the ratio varies (3.2–4.3×).

[MEASURED] (Argine README; the RAM-percentage runs are not separately labelled in the logs) RAM peak with mmap 105-111% of the declared budget; with the store 79-88%.

## Interpretation

[DERIVED] The path, not the device, limited mmap streaming. This motivated the expert store (patch 0002) and is the reason a hard budget exists at all.

[SUPPORTED] **Audit closure (2026-10-11).** On the reference machine, the mmap/page-fault access path, rather than raw NVMe sequential bandwidth, was a major streaming bottleneck. Scope: the reference machine (i5-1340P, its NVMe, Windows 11), the tested runtime configuration, and the tested access pattern (896 KiB / 2.75 MiB slices, cold cache). Not generalised to other systems or storage. Strong historical result; no rerun required for closure ([historical audit](../../docs/research/HISTORICAL-AUDIT-EXP001-006.md)).

## Limitations

[LIMITATION] Device curves are specific to this NVMe; diskbench measures the device with a barrier pattern, not the full runtime.
